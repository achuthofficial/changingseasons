HOW TO SET UP A META / WHATSAPP BUSINESS ACCOUNT
For: automated "order ready" WhatsApp messages
(feeds into supabase/functions/send-whatsapp-order-ready/index.ts)

This only covers getting the Meta/WhatsApp side ready and the three things
you need to hand back. Deploying the Edge Function itself is documented in
the comment block at the top of that file already.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Facebook account
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
You need a normal personal Facebook account to sign in to Meta for
Developers. If you don't already have one (or don't want to use a personal
one), create a fresh one just for this — it doesn't need to be public or
used for anything else.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — Create a Meta Business Account
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Business name: Changing Seasons
4. Your name + work email (use the boutique's business email if you have
   one, otherwise your own is fine)
5. Fill in the business details it asks for (address, phone) — use the
   real boutique address (Road No. 10, Banjara Hills, SB Khan building,
   Hyderabad), since this may be checked later during business
   verification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Create a Meta App
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps
2. Click "Create App"
3. Choose app type: "Business"
4. App name: something like "Changing Seasons Notifications"
5. Link it to the Business Account you created in Step 2 when asked
6. Once created, you'll land on the App Dashboard

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — Add the WhatsApp product
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the App Dashboard, find "Add Product" and add "WhatsApp"
2. This opens WhatsApp > API Setup — Meta automatically gives you a FREE
   TEST phone number here. You can use this test number to try everything
   end-to-end before adding your real business number.
3. On this same page you'll see:
     - "Phone number ID"       <- this is one of the 3 things I need
     - "WhatsApp Business Account ID"
   Copy the Phone number ID somewhere safe.
4. There's also a "Temporary access token" shown here — DO NOT use this
   one for the final setup, it expires in 24 hours. It's fine for a quick
   test message only. See Step 6 for the permanent one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — Add your real business phone number (when ready to go live)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Skip this while testing with the free test number. When ready for real
customers:
1. WhatsApp > API Setup > "Add phone number"
2. Use a phone number that is NOT currently active on WhatsApp or
   WhatsApp Business (the regular app) — you cannot use a number that's
   already logged into WhatsApp on someone's phone unless you migrate it
   away first. A spare SIM/landline/VOIP number is the usual choice.
3. Verify it via the SMS/call code Meta sends.
4. You'll likely be prompted to complete "Business Verification" at some
   point (uploading a business document — GST certificate, Udyam
   registration, or similar, matching the business name/address). This can
   take a day or two to review. Until it's done, you get a lower daily
   messaging limit, which is still plenty for a small boutique to start.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — Generate a PERMANENT access token
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The token shown in Step 4 expires in 24 hours — you need a permanent one
that doesn't expire, via a "System User":
1. Go to business.facebook.com > Business Settings (gear icon)
2. Users > System Users > "Add"
3. Name it something like "whatsapp-automation", role: Admin
4. Click "Add Assets" — assign it your WhatsApp Business Account (and the
   App from Step 3) with Full Control
5. Click "Generate New Token"
   - Select the App from Step 3
   - Token expiration: "Never"
   - Permissions: check "whatsapp_business_messaging" and
     "whatsapp_business_management"
6. Copy the generated token immediately — Meta only shows it once. This is
   the second thing I need.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 — Create and submit the message template for approval
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Any message YOU send first (not a reply to the customer) must use a
pre-approved template — this is a WhatsApp rule, not something we can skip.
1. Go to business.facebook.com > WhatsApp Manager > Message Templates
   (or from the App Dashboard: WhatsApp > Message Templates)
2. Click "Create Template"
3. Fill in EXACTLY this (the Edge Function is already written to expect
   this exact name and this exact number/order of placeholders):

     Name:      order_ready
     Category:  Utility
     Language:  English (US)
     Body:      Hi {{1}}, your order ORD-{{2}} ({{3}}) is ready for
                pickup at Changing Seasons. Thank you!

4. Submit for review. Utility-category templates are usually approved
   within a few minutes to 24 hours. You'll get an email/notification when
   it's approved (or rejected, with a reason — if rejected, tell me the
   reason and I'll adjust the wording).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 — Test it before going live (optional but recommended)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the WhatsApp > API Setup page there's a "Send test message" box — use
it to send yourself the approved template using the temporary token, just
to confirm the number and template actually work before we wire up the
real automation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO SEND ME WHEN DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Phone Number ID            (from Step 4)
2. Permanent access token     (from Step 6)
3. Confirmation that the "order_ready" template is approved (Step 7)

Once I have those, the only remaining work is deploying the Edge Function
and wiring it up in the Supabase dashboard (already documented in
supabase/functions/send-whatsapp-order-ready/index.ts) — no more setup on
your end besides that.
